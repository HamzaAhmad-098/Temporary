Gate & Barrier Automation

Parking Barriers

Parking barriers are an essential component in managing and controlling access to parking lots, garages and restricted areas. These automated systems regulate the entry and exit of vehicles, ensuring security, proper management of parking spaces and smooth traffic flow.

Request a quote 0309-0308262

How they work

  1. 01

    Automated operation

    Parking barriers are usually equipped with advanced automation that allows seamless operation. The barrier arm rises or lowers automatically when a vehicle approaches, triggered by sensors, ticketing systems or remote access controls.

  2. 02

    ANPR integration

    Some systems integrate with Automatic Number Plate Recognition technology, allowing frictionless access based on the vehicle’s registration plate.
