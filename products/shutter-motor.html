Functions

  1. 01

    All-copper motor

    The motor is made entirely of copper, giving it a higher capacity and more durable use.

  2. 02

    Overheat protection

    A built-in mechanism protects the motor: the power supply is switched off immediately if the temperature reaches 110 °C, and the motor works again once it has dropped to 70 °C.

  3. 03

    Mechanical limit switch

    Precisely and safely adjusts the door position in both the up and down directions, with a stability control feature that can be operated remotely.

  4. 04

    Manual button

    Up / Stop / Down control for opening and closing the rolling door.
