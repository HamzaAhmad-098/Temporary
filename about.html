About us

Let the automation take the strain.

Company profile

Manufacturer, importer and after-sales partner

Dynamic Drive is a professional manufacturer specialising in the R&D, manufacture, sales and after-sales service of motors and their components.

Dynamic Traders® imports automation motors, industrial gear motors and industrial VFDs (inverters) — shutter motors, swing gate motors, sliding gate motors, tubular motors, central mechanical motors, sliding gear racks and all accessories — from all over the world.

Our range includes

  • AC shutter motors
  • DC shutter motors
  • AC sliding gate motors
  • DC sliding gate motors
  • Explosion-proof shutter motors
  • Swing gate motors
  • Tubular motors
  • Central mechanical motors
  • …and more

Our principle

“Quality without Compromise”

Long service life, up-to-date technologies and extreme quality are the main features of our products, and they keep Dynamic Drives standing out from the crowd. Our passion, coupled with our expertise, drives us to deliver better results and to stay at the forefront of our industry.

Based in Shenzhen, China — the city of automation — we aim to be the benchmark of quality in the roller shutter industry.
